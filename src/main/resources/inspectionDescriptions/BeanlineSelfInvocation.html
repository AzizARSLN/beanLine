Reports calls inside a bean to a method of the same bean when the called method relies on a container annotation. A direct call does not pass through the container proxy, so @Transactional, @TransactionAttribute, @Asynchronous, @Async, cache and retry annotations do not apply to it.

A transaction annotation is not reported when the caller already has the same transaction settings.

To fix this, move the method to another bean or call it through a proxy reference, such as an injected self reference or SessionContext.getBusinessObject().
