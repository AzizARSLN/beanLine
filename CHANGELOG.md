# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-01)

- Endpoint search for JAX-RS and Spring MVC, including inherited endpoints.
- Go to Endpoint action that reads the URL at the caret, in the selection or on the clipboard.
- Gutter navigation between beans and their injection points.
- Inspections for proxy self-invocation, proxy-inaccessible methods and `new` on managed beans.
